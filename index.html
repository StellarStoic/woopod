Library & subscriptions7 capabilities
Available

Direct RSS & Atom subscriptions

Paste a secure feed URL or supported Apple Podcasts link, preview every playable episode, then subscribe. The publisher feed remains the source of truth.

Available

Complete, refreshable episode lists

Pull down from the top of Library to check every active and archived subscription with live progress. New entries merge without discarding older episodes omitted by shortened feeds. Episode numbers appear before titles, and search narrows shows or episodes locally. Android Back or a tap on the selected Library button returns from an open podcast to its Library or Archived list.

Available

Archive without unsubscribing

Move a quiet subscription out of the everyday Library while preserving its catalog, progress, artwork, offline files, queue entries and OPML export. Restore it at any time.

Available

Artwork that arrives quickly

List thumbnails, preview art and Player art use separate sizes and app-private caching. Import a local image to override a show’s cover; restore the publisher image later.

Available

Per-podcast settings

Hold a Library podcast for Info, Pin to top, Priority or Unsubscribe, and hold an action to reorder the menu. The near-full-screen podcast settings also include priority, artwork override, default playback speed, automatic offline retention and archive/restore. Pins, priority and menu order are included in full backups.

Available

Listening prompts

Short original lines rotate while the Library overview is visible. Choose Another quote from the Library menu. No quote service or network request is involved.

Available

Intentional deletion

The red Danger zone asks twice, offers a full backup and clearly separates subscription deletion from queue, progress, appearance and identity.

Listening & playback11 capabilities
Available

Background playback & resume

Media3 keeps audio playing outside the app. Position checkpoints restore listening after restart, and the mini-player returns you to the full Player. Its long episode title and the podcast name in Player share Playlist’s title scroll speed setting; 0 stops movement on all three.

Available

Precise controls

Play/pause, seek, 15-second rewind and 30-second forward use large theme-colored targets. Drag the position dot immediately or tap a point to seek. A swipe starting elsewhere on the progress bar changes pages; cancelled gestures do not change playback. During buffering, an indicator temporarily replaces the timeline; the seek bar returns when playback is ready. Retry and playback errors stay visible.

Available

Speed, globally or per show

Adjust from 0.5× to 2.5× in Player’s top-right menu. A podcast can retain its own default and apply it as episodes change.

Available

Standalone Playlist

Equal-height cards reserve two title lines. Long titles scroll left on the second line; Settings controls speed from 0 (off) to 100. Tap a card to play; hold for Move to top/bottom, Favourite, Played, Subscribe/Unsubscribe and Remove. Unsubscribe asks for confirmation; new subscriptions appear at the top of Library. Hold an action or episode handle for 1.5 seconds to reorder.

Available

Played history that stays useful

Mark episodes manually or let completion do it. Library and Archived mark individual episodes without adding noise to podcast cards; Discover can identify previously heard shows. Durable local history survives unsubscribe and can be reversed without changing progress.

Available

A Player that fits

The poster adapts to available height so the episode title, timeline, transport buttons and action rail remain together on one non-scrolling screen. Tap a bookmark marker on the timeline to edit a Markdown note. Optional shake-to-bookmark saves a chosen number of seconds before the gesture, and its settings and notes are included in full backups. Tap the podcast name under Now playing to open its Library page.

Available

Sort your playlist

Sort by publication date, podcast priority, duration, download date, podcast name or publisher network. Tap the same sort again to reverse it; hold and drag the sort choices to reorder the menu. Set podcast priority from 0 to 9 in podcast settings, the Library podcast long-press menu, or an episode’s Playlist long-press menu. Zero means no priority; 1 is highest, 9 lowest, and unprioritized shows sort last. See how many subscriptions use each priority as you choose it. Priorities and menu order are included in full backups. Network grouping requires a publisher identifier in the podcast feed; unavailable values stay at the end.

Available

Timestamped bookmark notes

Save a private thought at the current playback time, using the five-second rewind and play/pause controls to find the right moment. Browse all bookmarks from Library’s menu, or search them locally in Discover. The editor offers Save, Clip and Delete; a clip starts at the bookmark time with its title as the initial public note. Bookmark notes and episode metadata are included in full backups.

Partial

Create a clip link

In Player, tap Scissors. Drag the timeline’s start/end handles, select Start or End, then slide the ruler for fine adjustment. Enter exact times reveals optional seconds or mm:ss input with millisecond precision; Use current position applies to the selected endpoint. Choose any valid portion of the episode—there is no clip-length cap. Woopod reads the actual episode length first; the timeline, fine ruler and typed times stop at that end. Exact times display as mm:ss or hh:mm:ss, and length reads in hours/minutes/seconds. Listen with restart/play-pause or check the final five seconds. A thin green playhead follows the audio on the slider in both the editor and preview, remaining at its position when paused. Create clip opens an editable local preview; Share and Copy share link are available there once sharing details are ready. Comments allow up to 280 Unicode characters and 1,024 UTF-8 bytes. The link includes the feed address and public comment, so use public podcasts only. Older episodes automatically recover their feed from saved details or podcast directories and cache it for next time. If recovery fails, the visible header offers feed entry while local preview still works. Browser playback depends on the companion deployment; creating a link does not post a message or upload audio.

Partial

Player action rail

V4V opens the episode’s payment-service links, other support links and crypto destinations in a large window with visual separation and no group headings. Publisher names and routing details remain visible; node keys are copyable rather than treated as invoices. The comment icon opens public episode conversations using Fountain-compatible signed notes and standard podcast identity tags. Read and reply with your connected account; relay confirmations are shown individually. Podcast discussions have a Comments button on the podcast page and are also available in episode details and podcast settings. Pull down the episode list to refresh its feed. Publisher discussions remain separately accessible. Share opens Android’s share sheet with a full-episode Woopod player link. The podcast page also has a Share icon for its Woopod podcast page, with expandable episode details and links to the full player. Woopod can also open shared podcast, episode and clip links when Android routes them to the app. Clips play within their selected times; automatic routing requires the release domain verification setup. Shared podcast, episode and clip links open on woopod.com with social preview cards where supported. Scissors creates unlimited-length timed clips with native listening and an editable preview before sharing. The browser player plays within the selected clip boundaries; visibility in other podcast apps can vary.

Available

Descriptions & chapters

Player shows the current chapter title beneath the episode title and follows publisher-provided chapter artwork as you listen. It falls back to the episode or podcast cover when needed. Open episode Info for show notes and chapters; tap a chapter time to play from that point. Inline chapters, timestamps and Podcasting 2.0 JSON chapter files are supported.

Partial

Richer listening context

Inline chapters, JSON chapter files, descriptions and timestamped private notes work now. Embedded media chapters and transcripts remain to be added.

Audio tuning7 capabilities
Available

Skip silence

Use the switch below playback speed in Player’s three-dot menu, or You → Audio, to shorten quiet gaps while preserving speech speed. Both controls share one saved setting that applies immediately to streamed and downloaded episodes; all audio effects default off.

Available

Rewind after interruption

Choose Off or rewind 0.5–3 seconds in half-second steps when playback resumes after a temporary audio-focus interruption. Notifications that play without interrupting audio do not trigger it. The setting is included in backups.

Available

Sound between episodes

An optional short chime marks automatic advancement to a different queued episode, with a brief pause before the next episode resumes. Manual selection and replay do not chime.

Available

Volume boost

Raise quiet recordings by 0–12 dB. Peak limiting protects against digital clipping; strong boost can reduce dynamics. Automatic loudness leveling remains future work.

Available

Mono audio

Combine all channels so either ear receives the full conversation. Mono takes precedence over surround and disables its slider while enabled.

Available

Five-band equalizer & presets

Choose from 20 saved curves: Flat, Speech, Warm Voice, Vocal Clarity, Rock, Pop, Jazz, Classical, Acoustic, Electronic, Dance, Hip-Hop, R&B, Metal, Bass Boost, Treble Boost, Bass Cut, Treble Cut, Loudness and Late Night. Adjusting a band marks the curve Custom. The five sliders span −12 to +12 dB; presets keep your other audio controls unchanged.

Available

Bass

A separate 0–12 dB bass control works with the equalizer on or off. Headroom compensation keeps tonal changes from blindly increasing already loud peaks.

Available

Surround / stereo width

Widen stereo sound from 0–100%, best heard through headphones. Centred voices stay centred; mono recordings cannot gain missing stereo detail. Reset audio returns all these controls to their original defaults.

Discovery5 capabilities
Available

Podcast, episode and people search

One field accepts search terms or a feed link. Tap the podcast, episode, bookmark and people counts to independently show or hide each group. People matches are episodes associated with the searched name in Podcast Index, including indexed person tags. Not every appearance is tagged. Episode results name their show or station feed. The podcast preview fills 99% of the safe screen, and a substantially different feed title requires confirmation before subscribing.

Available

Multiple independent sources

Compact cards show results from Podcast Index, gPodder, fyyd and Apple across multiple storefronts. Tap a card for details or hold it for actions; hold and drag actions to reorder them. Podcast Index also finds episodes associated with people.

Available

Dig deeper

Continue into more regions, additional directory pages and up to five publisher RSS archives per pass. Search checks available episode descriptions and can discover a podcast through a matching episode. A podcast preview lists matching episodes first, highlighted in your theme color, followed by all episodes.

Available

New horizons by default

Saved subscriptions stay out of Discover unless Include subscriptions is enabled from the three-dot menu. Search your existing collection in Library.

Roadmap

Curated paths

Categories, suggestions and verified author/person discovery are planned. Current discovery is keyword and feed-link based.

Offline & cleanup5 capabilities
Available

Download any episode

Save audio for local playback. Download from Library, episode details, Discover or a show preview to add the episode to Playlist. A compact status strip opens transfer details, including speed and estimated time when available. If Android rejects a long redirect chain, Woopod can resolve up to 20 secure redirects and retry on a permitted connection. Ready files replace the network source; partial, failed and binned files are not played as downloads.

Available

Keep the latest N

Set 1–100 newest episodes per podcast, or zero to disable. Replacements must finish before older files move to the Bin, and the current Player item is protected.

Available

Network rules

Wi-Fi-only is the default. Cellular can be allowed deliberately; roaming downloads remain disabled.

Available

Flexible checks

Choose manual, 1/3/6/12/24-hour intervals, or a daily local time. Android may delay background work for battery or network conditions.

Available

A recoverable Playlist Bin

Playlist’s three-dot menu offers Update and an Autoplay switch, also available in Settings. A manually selected episode finishes before Autoplay continues from the top of Playlist. Completed automatic downloads are appended without interrupting playback. Its long-press action “Remove and delete” moves an episode to the Bin. In Settings, choose whether fully listened episodes move there automatically. Downloaded audio and streaming episodes can be restored to Playlist. Restoring an episode resets playback to the beginning while preserving its played history. A shared slider in Settings and Bin keeps files for 15 min, 30 min, 1 h, 6 h, 12 h, 1 d, 3 d or 7 d.

User & identity7 capabilities
Available

Create an identity on-device

Choose a display name, receive a random DiceBear Stack avatar, and generate a fresh secp256k1 key locally. The default profile description is “#woopod clan.”

Available

Explicit secret backup

The nsec stays hidden and red until requested. A secure dialog explains that there is no password reset, supports user-triggered copying and recommends a password manager.

Available

Connect an Android signer

Amber/NIP-55 connection keeps the signing key in the signer app. Woopod can display the public identity without silently requesting event-signing permission.

Available

Portable public profile and relays

Verified profiles are read from Woopod, purplepag.es and any additional relays you add under You → Nostr. Locally created accounts can publish to them with per-relay acknowledgements; public comments also use your added relays. The section explains what is public, what remains local and how to control your data. Additional relay settings are backed up; encrypted relay backups remain on Woopod.

Partial

Publisher comments

Tap the chat icon in Player or Episode comments in an episode’s details. Feed-provided discussion links choose the publisher’s official conversation. Woopod reads public Mastodon-compatible posts and replies with loading, retry, refresh and back-to-top controls. Content warnings hide the post text until revealed. Replies open on the original service using your account there. Other services use external links; feeds without discussion metadata explain that no thread is available. Publisher-disabled comments are respected. This is separate from the standard, public Nostr conversation shared with compatible podcast apps.

Available

Readable public conversations

Comments use nearly the whole Android screen. Authors show verified public profile names and pictures; tap one for their biography. Profile mentions and referenced notes open in-app previews, with browser fallback for unavailable records. Public notes and replies use the connected account only after an explicit posting action.

Roadmap

More identity-powered actions

Remote QR sign-in, private-key import, reviews and per-show completion posts remain planned. Listening is not published automatically.

Transfer, data & independence5 capabilities
Available

OPML in and out

Import nested subscription files or export a standard OPML 2.0 document through Android’s picker. Duplicates are skipped; large imports checkpoint progress and can resume.

Available

Data backup & relay backup

Save settings, subscriptions, per-show options, Playlist, progress and bookmarks as a compressed JSON file or an encrypted account-bound Woopod relay backup. Local files are not encrypted; keep them safe. Audio, artwork, font files, cached episode lists and private keys are excluded. Restore asks for confirmation and finishes after reopening Woopod. Relay backups are limited to 90 MB after compression.

Available

Large-library storage

Podcast and episode records live in incremental SQLite storage rather than one giant preference value. Library opens from small saved headers and accurate episode counts; episode archives load in the background, with direct loading when a podcast is opened. Migration is transactional, and header-only metadata edits retain episode catalogs.

Available

No required Google runtime

The app includes no Google identity, Firebase, ad or tracking SDK. AndroidX, Media3, system DownloadManager and WorkManager do not require a Google account to run.

Roadmap

Expanded ecosystem

Android creates clip links for the Woopod browser player. Listening statistics, badges, widgets, radio, alarms and advanced social discovery remain future work.

05 / Independence

Your phone.
Your feeds.
Your choice.

Woopod is designed to work without an account gate, ad network or required Google runtime. Public profile actions are separate from local listening. Nothing is published merely because you pressed play.

  • Direct publisher feedsRSS and Atom stay usable without a directory.
  • Private by defaultProgress, queue, settings and downloads remain local.
  • Portable subscriptionsStandard OPML import and export through your chosen storage.
